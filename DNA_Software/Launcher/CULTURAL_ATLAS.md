# Cultural atlas

The atlas displays source-labelled ancestry estimates with geographic and cultural context. Country boundaries and cultural motifs are not genetic boundaries.

Regional text, map data and photographs retain their source and licence information. Country artwork coverage varies; custom images can be added through the image assistant. Uploaded artwork is not genetic evidence.

Natural Earth outlines are public-domain geographic data. Refer to THIRD_PARTY.md and the artwork manifests for attribution.
