What do these results mean?

This suite compares local profiles, has no access to the global GEDmatch database and does not adopt proprietary Tier 1 models.

  • SNP genotype match (IBS) is not a shared DNA portion.
  • Segment import does not mean that IBD or personal assignment have been independently confirmed.
  • Overlapping segments are not blindly added to a cM total.
  • Triangulation and IBD2 candidates require additional testing of phase, marker coverage and assignment.
  • Ancestry and relatedness require appropriate references. ROH alone does not establish an exact parental relationship.
