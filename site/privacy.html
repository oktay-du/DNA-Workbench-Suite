Privacy

Updated 5 November 2026. Project contact: Oktay Du / OD · oktaydu@proton.me. GitHub project owner.

Local application

DNA Workbench processes imported DNA, reports, family records and results on your computer by default. The app does not automatically upload DNA, reports, usage telemetry or crash reports to OD. There is no project-operated DNA hosting account. Profiles, databases, model files, local chat history, caches and job logs remain on your drive until you delete them; backups can contain further copies.

Downloads, update checks and optional integrations contact their respective providers. These requests disclose ordinary connection information such as your IP address. Optional external AI is disabled until configured and requires review/approval of the outbound payload. Running a third-party application or uploading through it may disclose data under that provider's terms. Operating-system, browser, WSL, package-manager and tool policies are separate; this project does not promise that every external component is offline or telemetry-free.

Feedback and crash reports

Nothing is automatically sent to GitHub. Feedback drafts are prepared locally. Optional diagnostics are off by default and retain basic app/OS details and recognized exception types only. They exclude raw log messages, commands, profile names, paths, genotypes, report contents and credentials. A minimal native crash record contains an exception type and time, without the exception message. Review the editable draft yourself before posting. Screenshot preview is local; attaching it on GitHub is a separate manual action.

GitHub issues are public. OD, collaborators and other visitors can read anything you submit, including text, attachments and your GitHub identity. Do not submit DNA, medical documents, credentials or identifying information. Opening GitHub or copying a draft does not submit it. Do not include sensitive material in public privacy requests; contact oktaydu@proton.me without sending DNA or medical files.

Project website and support links

The static GitHub Pages website has no DNA upload, project analytics, advertising scripts or external font loader. GitHub documents that it logs visitors' IP addresses for security, including visitors who are not signed in. GitHub Pages data collection, GitHub privacy statement.

Donation and affiliate links lead to third-party sites. Their privacy, payment, cookie and account terms apply there. OD may receive information the payment provider makes available to recipients. Donations are optional; no claim of anonymous payment is made.

International privacy rights

Genetic and health information can require heightened protection. An operator who processes other people's data must assess their own lawful basis, consent/authorization, security, retention and disclosure obligations. A local-first design does not automatically exempt an operator or maintainer from applicable law. GDPR rights and controller/processor duties apply where their conditions are met; US federal/state privacy duties depend on the entities and processing involved. Türkiye KVKK may also apply, including information duties under Article 10. This notice does not waive rights, declare universal GDPR/KVKK/HIPAA/US compliance or eliminate legal duties. KVKK official information duties.

Your personal DNA and reports are not licensed under the software license. You retain applicable rights in your data. The project has no access to local files unless you deliberately share them through another channel. Sharing real data publicly is not required for support.

References: GDPR Articles 2, 4, 5, 6, 9 and 13, US FTC health privacy guidance, HHS HIPAA covered entities.

For privacy questions or requests about information actually received by the project, contact oktaydu@proton.me. State which interaction is concerned; do not attach sensitive DNA or medical records. Applicable statutory response and deletion duties remain unaffected. The project cannot erase copies independently held by public-site visitors.
